Wording by Yocheved, Sep 4

A virtual singles event that feels in person.

Walk around, meet people, have real conversations, and see who you click with. All without having to be in the same city.

Reserve a spotPeek into the room
1

Walk into the room

When the event starts, you enter a shared virtual room as an avatar that actually looks like you. Walk around, see who's there, and mingle. Everyone who meets your criteria is highlighted.

2

Ask someone to talk

See someone you want to meet? Click on them to see real photos and some basic info. If you're interested, ask them to chat. If they're interested too, you'll jump into a private seven-minute video call, then head back into the room.

3

See who you clicked with

At the end of the event, you'll see everyone you talked to and privately choose who you'd like to hear from again. If the interest is mutual, you'll both get each other's contact info.

What makes it different

  • No scrolling, no swiping. Just in-person interaction, online.
  • You choose who you talk to. No algorithms or forced rotations. See someone you want to meet? Go talk to them.
  • No awkward rejections. Conversation requests are private, and so are your choices at the end of the event.
  • Everyone shows up together. Events happen at scheduled times, so you're walking into a room full of people who are actually there to meet.
  • Seven minutes, face to face. Enough time to see if there's a connection, then head back into the room and keep mingling.
  • No awkward escapes required. Every conversation is mutual and lasts seven minutes. No getting cornered, no searching for a friend to come rescue you.

Built for trust

Real people, real photos, live video. Accounts are verified and photos are reviewed before they're shown. Calls aren't recorded, block and report are always easy to reach, and your contact information stays private unless you mutually match.

First events

The first mixers will be invitation-only. Leave your email and we'll let you know when the first one is happening.
